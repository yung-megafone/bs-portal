B.S. Portal

Operational software without the surveillance baggage.

BSP is a Django/MySQL modular monolith for identity, departments, asset management, service ticketing, and timekeeping. The project favors first-party operational records, explicit workflow history, and self-hosting over advertising, behavioral tracking, or unnecessary employee telemetry.
